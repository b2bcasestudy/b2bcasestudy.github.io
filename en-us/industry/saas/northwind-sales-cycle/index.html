The Challenge

Northwind Analytics was closing $50k+ annual contracts with Fortune 500 prospects, but deal flow consistently stalled during late-stage procurement. Security teams and CFOs demanded real-world operational proof before signing off.

The Solution

We conducted deep-dive interviews with 5 of Northwind’s cornerstone enterprise clients, extracting hard metrics around data processing speed and compliance savings. We structured these insights into executive one-pagers designed specifically for procurement review decks.

"Having independently verified customer ROI transformed our sales collateral. It gave our AEs the exact firepower needed to clear CFO evaluation in days instead of weeks." — Laura Méndez, VP of Sales Ops @ Northwind

The Results

  • Average evaluation phase reduced from 72 days down to 45 days.
  • Demo-to-close conversion increased by 38% across US East Coast territories.
  • Generated $1.2M in new pipeline directly attributed to case study downloads.
