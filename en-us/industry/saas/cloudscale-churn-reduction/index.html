The Challenge

CloudScale experienced rapid new customer acquisition, but account expansion plateaued after month six. Mid-market engineering teams lacked clear post-onboarding benchmarks to justify renewal budget to leadership.

The Solution

We created a series of peer-focused implementation case studies documenting step-by-step infrastructure optimizations and cost savings achieved by peer DevOps leaders.

"Our CSM team now sends these case studies at month four of every contract. It sets clear expectations and gives client champions the metrics they need for internal renewal approvals." — Marcus Vance, Head of Customer Success @ CloudScale

The Results

  • Gross account churn decreased by 42% year-over-year.
  • Net Retention Rate (NRR) climbed from 98% to 118%.
  • Saved an estimated $3.4M in annual recurring revenue (ARR).
