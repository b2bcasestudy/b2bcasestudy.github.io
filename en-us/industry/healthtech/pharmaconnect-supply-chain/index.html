The Supply Chain Risk

Specialty biologics and vaccines require strict temperature ranges during transport. Manual verification processes led to shipping spoilage and delayed compliance reporting under the FDA's Drug Supply Chain Security Act (DSCSA).

The IoT Automation Platform

PharmaConnect deployed cellular IoT sensors and automated electronic serialization records, providing instant temperature audit trails from manufacturer warehouse to clinical lab receipt.

Key Outcomes

  • Reduced order-to-delivery fulfillment time by 79% across 120 clinical partner labs.
  • Eliminated $2.1M in annual cold-chain product spoilage losses.
  • Achieved 100% automated compliance with FDA DSCSA electronic interoperability rules.
