The Legacy Challenge

Operating on aging on-premise servers created catastrophic query latency during peak clinical shifts. Regional health networks risked severe regulatory penalties and compromised patient care without a secure, cloud-native architecture.

The Migration Architecture

MedData implemented an automated, encrypted HL7/FHIR data pipeline to migrate 12M+ patient records to dedicated multi-region AWS HealthOmics and Azure for Healthcare vaults, backed by full Business Associate Agreements (BAA).

Measurable Outcomes

  • Reduced EHR patient record loading time from 8.4 seconds to 320 milliseconds.
  • Achieved 100% audit readiness under SOC2 Type II and HIPAA Security Rule mandates.
  • Lowered operational infrastructure costs by $1.4M annually across participating health networks.
