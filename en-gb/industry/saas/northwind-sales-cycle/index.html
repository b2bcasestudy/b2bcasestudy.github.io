The Challenge

Selling enterprise analytics platforms into UK Tier-1 financial institutions and public sector contractors involves navigating strict Information Security, FCA compliance checks, and multi-stakeholder procurement boards. Northwind Analytics faced prolonged deal cycles averaging 114 days, primarily stalled during legal and compliance review.

The Solution

Northwind restructured its sales asset architecture, deploying localized customer verification reports, SOC2/ISO27001 assurance packs, and clear risk-mitigation data sheets tailored specifically to UK procurement standards.

Key Results

  • Reduced deal cycle length from 114 days to 87 days (-27 days).
  • Unlocked £1.1M in expansion opportunities across London and Edinburgh financial hubs.
  • Achieved 100% compliance approval rate on first-pass procurement reviews.
